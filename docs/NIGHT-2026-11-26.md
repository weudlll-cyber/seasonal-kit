  parts); the new specs measure each fixture at the width of the window, not the page.
- Mini Games' `isPublicAddress` misses `[::ffff:7f00:1]` (hex-mapped IPv4); its cookie outlives a
  website session by 23 days. Both are read-only from here; the kit's versions do not have them.
- The scratch consumers of the two delivery proofs of v0.21.0 and v0.22.0 are still in the session's
  scratch folder: deleting them was refused by the permission mode.
