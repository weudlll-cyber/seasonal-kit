## Piece 1: back link space

collapsed. A `gap: 0.35em` keeps them apart. showcase/layout.spec.ts (new) measures, in Chromium,
the distance from the arrow's box to the words' text box (a Range): 4 px or more. Without the
fix it measures 0 (checked). DECISIONS K32 records that the games' requests go into the kit.
