## Addendum: two sweep additions (v0.38.0)

#68 was released when the addendum arrived, so this is the next minor, on its own branch
`feat/sweep-setup-floors`.

- A screen's `setup`, run once before its looks (K115). The handbook names the request limit as a
  trap of the sweep; the limit is not loosened.
- A screen's `floors` of visible items (K116), each with the decision's number. They hold at 16 px
  unless a floor names other fonts, because the decisions behind them speak of normal text. A
  floor that no look checks stops the sweep.
- Checks: typecheck, lint of `src`, `showcase` and `tests`; `src/testing` and `tests/` once (29
  files, 483 passed); `showcase/testing.spec.ts` once (10 passed). No full showcase run.
- "Fully visible" is measured by geometry: inside the window and every scrolling or clipping box.
  A part drawn over an item (an open sheet, a sticky band) does not take it away.
